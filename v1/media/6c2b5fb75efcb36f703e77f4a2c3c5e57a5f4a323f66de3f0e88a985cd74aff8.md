# Notion

Bring your Notion pages and databases into conversations with Grain.

## What you can do

- Find and read pages you have access to.
- Summarize notes and organize workspace content.
- Create or edit pages and database entries through the available tools.

Try asking:

> Find my project notes and summarize the next steps.

> Create a test page with the meeting notes we just discussed.

Available actions depend on Notion's tools, your account permissions and workspace
policies. Execution follows Grain's configured Agent settings.

## Account access

Sign in with your Notion account. The connection acts with your Notion permissions;
workspace administrators may restrict access. Use a disposable workspace for
testing. Disconnecting in Grain removes its stored account access; you can also
manage connected tools in Notion.

Requests go to Notion's hosted server. Information returned by tools is processed
by the model configured for Grain's Agent. The extension does not receive Grain
screen, selected-text, dictation-history or prompt-settings access.

Maintained by Grain using Notion's official service. Its name and artwork identify
the integration and do not imply endorsement.
